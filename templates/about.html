About Me

High School Technologist

I am a high school technologist working at the intersection of machine learning, software engineering, and neuroscience, driven by a deep curiosity about how computational models can meaningfully represent, analyze, and augment human cognition. My interests center on using AI not just as a performance-driven tool, but as a framework for understanding complex neural and cognitive systems. I focus on developing AI-driven solutions that are both technically rigorous and ethically grounded, applying machine learning to multimodal data, neural signals, and cognitively relevant representations. My work emphasizes interpretability, real-world applicability, and responsible system design, especially in contexts where AI directly interacts with human learning, reasoning, or behavior.

I focus on developing AI-driven solutions that are both powerful and ethically grounded, applying machine learning to multimodal data, neural analysis, and intelligent systems that have real-world impact.

My long-term goal is to create tools and platforms that bridge technology and neuroscience—amplifying human learning, decision-making, and creativity—while advancing brain-inspired computing, neuro-aligned machine intelligence, and AI-assisted cognitive analysis.
