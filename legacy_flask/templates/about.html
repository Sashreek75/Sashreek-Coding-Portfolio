About Me

Student Technologist & Independent Researcher

I'm a student technologist and independent researcher who builds AI-driven systems that augment human potential. My work sits at the intersection of machine learning, software engineering, and human-centered design, with a particular emphasis on building end-to-end platforms that adapt to individuals rather than forcing people into standardized frameworks.

Many existing systems evaluate people through deficit-based metrics — grades, standardized tests, rigid benchmarks. I'm interested in a different paradigm: strength-oriented intelligence systems that identify advantages and leverage them. This philosophy drives the platforms I build and the research questions I pursue.

Across multiple projects, I explore a core idea: the most powerful technology is not technology that replaces humans, but technology that amplifies the unique strengths of each individual mind. My long-term ambition is to build influential adaptive AI systems, develop reliable AI infrastructure, and ship tools that make technology more aligned with human diversity.

Adaptive AI Systems
Building ML-powered platforms that model users and dynamically adjust to their strengths
End-to-End Development
Shipping full-stack AI applications — from model experimentation to deployed, interactive platforms
AI Infrastructure
Building tools for dataset reliability, bias detection, and reproducibility in ML research
Systems Thinking
Solving structural problems — broken education models, unreliable datasets, static learning platforms
